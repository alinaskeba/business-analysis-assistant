# Draft expectations: meeting rooms with a team

Status: DRAFT, not approved. Split: dev. This is an English translated adaptation of the original draft.
Use the [English brief](../en/with-team/brief.txt) and the [complete 36-row table](../en/expectations.draft.md).
The original Ukrainian draft is preserved in history/pre-english at the same project-relative path.

Expected changes after adding the team: preserve Product Owner, Business Analyst, Full-stack Developer,
QA Engineer and office manager, with all duties and verbatim English evidence. PO sets priorities,
decides booking rules and decides launch. BA gathers requirements, prepares the specification and agrees
it with PO. Developer implements and deploys; QA tests including simultaneous bookings of the same room;
office manager advises on room-use rules and participates in acceptance testing.
Do not keep an unknown-team gap when the team is explicitly described.

Preserve the goal, Employee/Administrator/Office manager process roles, available-room browsing by date
and time, creating/cancelling own bookings, adding rooms with capacity/equipment, same-room overlap
prevention, corporate-email sign-in, email confirmations and the one-month deadline.
The conflicting statements remain:
- All bookings are confirmed automatically and immediately.
- Every booking must be approved by the office manager before confirmation.

Store both as separate requirements, link the conflict to their IDs and ask a blocking neutral question.
PO authority does not resolve the conflict. Do not infer product access for the team or equate PO with
Administrator. Time zone, duration rules, sign-in method and measurable success remain unresolved.
Do not generate a specification, tasks or RACI during this analysis stage.
