We need a small web application for booking meeting rooms in our office.
The goal is to reduce scheduling clashes in bookings.
An employee views available rooms by date and time, creates and cancels their own booking.
An administrator adds rooms, their capacity and equipment.
The system does not allow the same room to be booked for a time that overlaps another booking.
All bookings are confirmed automatically and immediately.
Every booking must be approved by the office manager before confirmation.
Sign-in uses corporate email. Email booking confirmations are required.
We want the first version in one month.

A small team is building the internal meeting-room booking application. The first version is planned in one month.
The Product Owner from the administrative department sets priorities and makes decisions about booking rules.
The Business Analyst gathers requirements, prepares the specification and agrees it with the Product Owner.
The Full-stack Developer is responsible for technical implementation and deployment.
The QA Engineer is responsible for testing, including checking simultaneous bookings of the same room.
The office manager advises on room-use rules and participates in acceptance testing.
The Product Owner makes the decision to launch the first version.
